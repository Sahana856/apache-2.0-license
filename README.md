# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a positive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and polite.
- Be welcoming to everyone.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Avoid harassment and discrimination.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces and interactions.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behaviour may result in warnings or removal from the community.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Community leaders will review reported incidents fairly and take appropriate action.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
